About me

I am a PhD student at the University of Lille and Inria, working in the field of human-computer interaction and haptics. My research focuses on collaborative design of vibrotactile experiences in mixed reality, with a particular interest in understanding how people collaborate when designing and authoring haptic feedback.

Previously, I worked on robotics, automation, IoT, and haptic interaction. I received my Master's degree in Robotics and Automation after completing my Bachelor's degree in Mechanical Engineering.

Outside of research, I enjoy running, exploring new technologies, and learning about human-computer interaction.

Research

My research investigates collaborative haptic design in mixed reality. I am particularly interested in how haptic designers communicate, coordinate, and cooperate when creating vibrotactile experiences.

My current work explores the challenges faced by haptic designers during collaborative design processes and investigates how asymmetric collaboration can support the authoring of haptic experiences in virtual reality.
